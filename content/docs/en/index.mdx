The One is a desktop app for macOS and Windows by The Zeroth. You tell its **main assistant** what you need. Anything bigger than one answer becomes a **matter** with its own board, and the assistant hands it to a **working group**, a team of AI agents that does the work on your own computer.

The Windows download is still 0.4.28, which has no main assistant or matters, so these docs follow the macOS app. The One also runs on a Linux machine without a screen: see [The One on a Linux server](./start/linux-server).

## What the sidebar holds

At the top of the sidebar, next to **The One**, is the Space you are using: a whole set of working-group templates, roles and Skills. Click it to switch to another Space. Below it, from top to bottom:

- **Conversation.** This is the main assistant. Every Space has one permanent assistant, and it is the first place to go: it answers what it can, opens matters for the rest, hands them to working groups and follows them up. Everything that waits for you is in the strip above the conversation, which says how many (for example **2 waiting for you**), with a button for each that takes you to its card; the count beside **Conversation** in the sidebar is the same number.
- **Panorama.** Every open matter on one page: each matter's working groups and their Agents, and whether they are thinking, working or waiting right now.
- **Matters.** A matter is a board of cards: what waits for you, what happened day by day, and the matter itself with its goal, working groups, checklists, links and files. A working group is a team of Agents built from a working-group template, a Graph. Its questions and deliveries arrive on the board as cards, and you can also talk to it directly.
- **Runs by itself.** Triggers keep recurring work going while you are away: schedules, webhooks, and new mail, orders, files or pages. A matter can have triggers of its own too. It opens **Settings › Runs by itself**.
- **Weekly report.** A look back at the last 7 days: what was delivered, how much passed first time, how often you were asked, what it cost and what it changed by itself.
- **Teams and tools.** The working groups the main assistant picks from, and the roles, Skills, prompts, external tools (MCP), models and Plugins your Space holds. In a Space of your own, the main assistant and the evolution lab can improve them too.
- **Settings.** Account and appearance, models and sign-in, what runs by itself, the phone channel, the action log and undo.

Until a model is set up, **Get started** shows under **Conversation**; when a Plugin brings an app of its own, **Apps** appears as well.

You do not have to go anywhere to catch up on what happened while you were away: on a day with news you have not seen, the main assistant greets you in the conversation with what happened since you last looked, and if the app is open at 8:00 and there is news, a system notification arrives too. See [Waiting for you and deliveries](./the-one-app/needs-you) and the [Weekly report](./the-one-app/weekly).

## Nothing is a black box

Multi-agent work is only worth trusting if you can see it. In The One, **Panorama** shows at a glance what every matter's working groups and Agents are doing right now, and clicking a working group opens its **Theater**. In a matter's Theater you can watch its working groups at work, read each of their Agents' messages, tool calls and context, and review each change block by block. Agents do not stop to ask permission for each step. Instead, everything they do is recorded in the **Action log**, and files they changed or deleted can be put back.

---
title: More Possibilities in Multi-Agent
description: Capabilities that single-Agent systems cannot naturally provide.
---

Multi-Agent systems have unique properties and possibilities that a single Agent cannot provide on its own.

- [Communication](./communication): how Agents pass work and information, inside a working group, between groups on a matter's board, and with you.
- [Context Editing](./context-editing): one Agent editing another Agent's context.
- [Dynamic Graph](./dynamic-graph): Agents create, clone, activate and put to sleep other Agents at runtime, growing their group as they work.
- [Meditation](./meditation): an Agent compresses its own history in place, before its context window runs out.
- [Self-Evolution](./self-evolution): how a Space improves itself from evidence, through the evolution matter and the nightly review.

These notes cover The One 0.5.1 on macOS. It follows 0.5.0 and keeps its shape: one main assistant per Space, and matters worked by working groups.

## Look

- Neutral black and white: the light look is 素白 (plain white), the dark look 石墨 (graphite); one sans typeface with softer corners; a sidebar that runs the window's full height.
- The conversation column grows with the window up to 1040px, and reads at 16px when the window is wide.
- The matter page is centered, with actions such as closing the matter in the title bar.

## Home

- Each day opens with the main assistant's greeting, and a line in the conversation names the day.
- Search the conversation from the page header or with Cmd+F.
- A handled card folds into one line where it was, and you can still change your mind there.
- A line above the composer says when the main assistant rests, or when a failed reply tries again.
- The conversation keeps its newest words in view until you scroll away yourself.

## Panorama

- **Panorama** in the sidebar shows every matter, working group and Agent on one page: **Org chart** shows who works on what right now, **Flow** shows today's timeline for each Agent.
- Each working group is drawn as the graph its template makes; Flow zooms to the last hour or three, and finished branches fold away.
- You can close a matter from the sidebar.

## Waiting for you and Needs you

- What waits for you is counted once, so the sidebar, the morning report and the Dock badge agree.
- Closing a matter takes its things out of Waiting for you and the morning report at once.
- Every waiting card names the working group it came from; a question from the main assistant is named by what it asks.
- The inbox page is called **Needs you** everywhere.

## Model connections

- When the official connection's allowance is used up, its cards and notices say so and open the subscription page, instead of asking you to sign in again.
- A key or account out of credit counts as a usage limit you are asked about, not as a refused sign-in.
- A failing Agent says when it retries, and you can retry it now.

## Settings and teams

- Settings come in six sections, and each page names its section above it.
- **Teams and tools** lists the working groups first, then what they work with.
- A developer view, off by default, holds what developers look at.

## Plugins

Plugins update in the Plugin library, independently of the app's version. Updated alongside 0.5.1:

- **Mailbox 0.2.0**: Google accounts sign in with Google to send and read Gmail, replacing Gmail Control; drafts can be saved; replying to mail you sent goes to the people it went to. The official Space's main assistant and General group now use Mailbox.
- **Computer Use 0.4.0**: on macOS it finds a window by its app's name and sees and controls any app. Give The One Accessibility and Screen Recording permission in System Settings first.
- **OneCanvas 0.5.0**: follows the app's theme and shows a project's design system; page items show an Agent's HTML page.
- **Image Generation 0.5.3**: portrait sizes need no capability manifest, and a Plugin update no longer stops the image studio over old pinned hashes.
- **Payments 0.2.0**, **Sites 0.2.0** and **Visual Check 0.1.2** enter the Plugin library for the first time; **Blender Control 0.7.0** brings a viewer panel and background tasks.
- The Plugin library reads which host interface a Plugin version needs, and tells you first when The One must be updated.

## Other changes

- Dates read in the interface language, with your system's region.
- More of the interface follows your language, including starting, signing in, the Plugin install confirmation window and the Phone channel.
- The [Linux server](../linux-server) installs and runs on Alpine and other musl systems.

title: Models and Pricing
description: How the official model service differs from your own providers, how membership plans and quota packs are charged, and who bills what.

The main assistant and the working groups in The One work with models. The models can come from the official model service that comes with a The One membership, or from a model service of your own. This page explains the difference and how each is charged; [Model connections](../the-one-app/settings/api-configuration) covers setting up every connection, and [Models](../the-one-app/capabilities/models) covers which Agent uses which model.

## Two ways

| | Official model service | Your own model service |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you connect | Subscribe to The One membership and the official connection appears by itself, with no API key to prepare | Sign in with ChatGPT (OpenAI Codex); add an API key for OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini; or use a **Custom Provider** for a service with an OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible API, your organisation's model gateway or models on your own computer |
| Which models | Models on OpenRouter, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral, DeepSeek and others | The models that service offers |
| How it is charged | From the usage your membership includes each month, then from your quota packs | By that service: an API key at its prices, Sign in with ChatGPT against the Codex allowance of your ChatGPT plan, an organisation's gateway by its own rules |
| Suits | Using The One for the first time, not wanting to manage keys, one bill for every model, trying many models | Accounts you already have, a team that requires its own accounts or gateway, models on your own computer, the lowest cost per call |

You can use both at once: the model preset an Agent uses decides which connection its requests take. Calls through your own service do not use any official model service usage.

## The official model service

The official model service comes with a The One membership and runs through OpenRouter. Signing in with a The Zeroth account alone does not bring it: without a membership, **Get started** offers **Become a member**. Once you subscribe, the official connection shows up in The One.

You can follow your remaining usage in the console, or in the app: in **Settings › Models and sign-in › Model connections**, the official connection's card shows your **Membership** tier, how much of the included usage is left, and **Manage subscription**.

This is a convenience, not a discount: no keys to prepare, and one bill for every model. If the lowest cost per call matters most, use your own provider keys.

## Membership plans and quota packs

Every plan has the same features; they differ only in how much official model service usage they include: Pro includes three times as much as Starter, and Max ten times as much.

| Plan | Price | Included usage per month | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $20 / month | 1× | Light usage, personal exploration, low-frequency Agent work |
| Pro | $60 / month | 3× | Daily usage, multi-agent workflows, higher task frequency |
| Max | $200 / month | 10× | Heavy usage, complex workflows, high-frequency automation |

Included usage resets at the start of each billing period and does not roll over. Once it is used up, the strip of what waits for you above the conversation, the matter board and the conversation say **The official connection's allowance is used up**, with a link to your account's subscription page, where you can upgrade your plan or buy the quota pack below.

For a month when you use more than usual and don't want to upgrade, buy a **quota pack** in the console, at $20 a purchase. A quota pack does not change your plan; it only adds usage.

- Calls use your plan's included usage first, and pack usage after that.
- For the same $20, a pack holds a little less usage than a plan includes. In exchange it does not expire: it does not reset with the billing period, and what is left still works after your plan ends.

**Settings › Models and sign-in › Model connections** holds the connections that Agents send their model requests through: the official connection that comes with a The One membership, your ChatGPT sign-in, API keys for OpenAI, Anthropic and Google Gemini, and any compatible service, including models running on your own computer. A connection only gives access: which model an Agent uses, and through which connection, is set in model presets or in its role. See [Models](../capabilities/models).

![Settings › Models and sign-in › Model connections with one ChatGPT sign-in](/docs-assets/images/0.5/en/api-configuration-overview.png)

## What the page shows

At the top are counts of your **Connections**, **OAuth** sign-ins and **Keys**. Below them comes the official connection, when your account has one, then **Connected**: every other connection with its provider, its protocol if it is a custom one, how it signs in (OAuth, API Key or No key) and its Base URL, with a mark on any that is disabled or still has no key. **Add connection**, at the top right, opens the list of providers.

Keys and sign-ins are kept on this computer, encrypted by the system. Definition Spaces and model presets only refer to a connection; they never hold its key.

## The official connection

The official connection comes with a The One membership. It shows as **Official OpenRouter** with the **Official** badge, and its card gives your **Membership** tier, how much of your plan's included usage is left, and **Manage subscription**, which opens your subscription in the account console in your browser. Your account provides this connection, so you cannot edit or delete it here. Once the included usage runs out, requests on the official connection are refused: the strip of what waits for you above the conversation, the matter board and the Agent's conversation say **The official connection's allowance is used up**, with a link to your account's subscription page, where you renew or top it up (see [When a provider refuses](#when-a-provider-refuses)).

If your account has no official connection, there is no card. During first use, **Get started** (**Start** in the sidebar) offers **Become a member** instead, or you connect a service of your own as below. Plans and usage are explained in [Models and Pricing](../../start/models-and-pricing).

## Sign in with ChatGPT

With a ChatGPT Plus or Pro subscription, Agents can use its Codex allowance without an API key. Choose **Add connection**, then **OAuth** on **OpenAI Codex**, and sign in on the page that opens in your browser. In Get started the same choice is called **Sign in with ChatGPT**.

The connection's row shows **ChatGPT plan usage**: how much of each limit window is used and when it resets. The allowance belongs to your ChatGPT account and is shared with any other Codex app you use. When the sign-in expires, choose **Sign in again** on the row.

## API keys

For OpenAI (Responses API), Anthropic (Claude Messages API) or Google Gemini, choose **Add connection**, then **Connect** on the provider, paste the key you created in its console and choose **Save Connection**. To change the key later, choose **Update** on the connection's row; leaving the key field empty keeps the saved key.

The Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Antigravity sign-ins of earlier versions were removed in 0.5.0; connect Anthropic or Gemini with an API key instead. See [The One 0.5.0 Release Notes](../../start/release-notes/0-5-0).

## Custom providers and models on your computer

A **Custom Provider** connects any service that speaks one of three protocols. Choose **Add connection**, then **Add** under **Custom**, and fill in:

- **Name**: what the connection is called in the list and in model presets.
- **Protocol**: **OpenAI Chat Completions** sends requests to `<Base URL>/chat/completions` and suits most compatible services; the Base URL usually ends in `/v1`. **OpenAI Responses** sends them to `<Base URL>/responses`; choose it only if the service implements that API. **Anthropic Messages** sends them to `<Base URL>/v1/messages`, so leave `/v1` off the Base URL: it is the same value you would give Claude Code as `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL`.
- **API Base URL**: the service's address.
- **Authentication**: **API key**, sent as `Authorization: Bearer` with the OpenAI protocols and as `x-api-key` with Anthropic Messages; or **No key**, for a server that takes no credentials, such as Ollama, LM Studio or vLLM on this computer. With No key, requests carry no key at all, not even one from the environment.

Two examples:

| What | Protocol | Base URL | Authentication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OpenRouter with your own key | OpenAI Chat Completions | `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1` | API key |
| Ollama on this computer | OpenAI Chat Completions | `http://127.0.0.1:11434/v1` | No key |

## Changes apply at once

Saving a connection takes effect without restarting the app. If the app says the backend could not reload, choose **Restart Backend** in Settings › General.

For Agents to use a connection, have a model preset go through it (in **Settings › Models and sign-in › Default model**), or choose it when you configure a model of its own in a role's **Model & Parameters**. The preset editor's **Refresh models** fetches the models the connection offers right now. See [Models](../capabilities/models) and [Roles](../capabilities/roles).

## Deleting a connection

Choose the trash icon on the connection's row. If no role names the connection directly, it is deleted at once, without a question. If roles do, a dialog lists them, and **Migrate and Delete** moves them to another available connection (or, when there is none, back to the default). Model presets that use the connection keep pointing at it, so give them another connection in Settings › Models and sign-in › Default model; any model capacity override you set for the connection is deleted with it.

## When a provider refuses

A provider can refuse in four ways, and each wants something different:

- **It rejects the key or sign-in** (HTTP 401): the Agent in [All runs](./all-runs) says the connection **rejected its credentials**, with an **Open this connection** link. When a matter's working group runs into it, the strip of what waits for you above the conversation also shows a **Needs your sign-in** card, whose **Fix it in Settings** brings you to the connection (see [Waiting for you and deliveries](../needs-you)). Until you update the key or sign in again, requests through that connection stay on this computer instead of reaching the provider; the Agent keeps retrying by itself, and the connection is checked again every 30 minutes. A new key or sign-in lets requests through at once, and **Retry agents awaiting recovery** in a run's **Run controls** tries again right away.
- **It denies access** (HTTP 403): the account may lack access to the model, or to the service from your region. The connection is not paused, and its card in Waiting for you says **Access denied**; check what the connection is allowed to use, or choose another model.
- **The credit runs out**: when a key's credit limit or the account's balance is used up, OpenRouter answers 402 or 403 and says which. That counts as usage used up, not as a rejected sign-in or denied access. The Agent keeps retrying by itself; the strip of what waits for you shows an **Out of credit** card, and the working groups on a matter's board and the Agent's conversation say so too. Add credit or raise the key's limit, or choose another connection, and the work carries on. The official connection's allowance comes from your membership, so its card and the other places offer **Open my subscription**, which opens your account's subscription page, where you renew or top it up. Once the official connection's allowance is used up, the account disables its key, so the provider rejects the key (HTTP 401). The One then asks your account how much is left: with 0% left it reads as the allowance used up, with a link to the subscription page; with some left it is a rejected key or sign-in as above.
- **Its policy blocks the request**: the Agent shows **The model provider blocked this request**, and the Agent Roster marks it **Needs action**. The same request is not sent again by itself, and **Recover all** and **Retry agents awaiting recovery** skip it. Edit the task to make clear what you are authorized to do, use an applicable, authorized model connection, or cancel the task. A changed request or another connection goes out as usual.

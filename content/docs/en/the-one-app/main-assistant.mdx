The main assistant is the conversation The One opens on, and the one place to bring anything you want done. It answers what it can at once, keeps bigger work as matters, hands that work to working groups and follows it until you are satisfied.

## One conversation that goes on

Every Space has its own main assistant: **Conversation** in the sidebar, **Main assistant** on the page. It is one permanent conversation that runs across days and across all your matters. There is no new chat to start and no session list to tidy up. Everything you ask lands in the same place, so the assistant can connect what you said last week with what you ask today.

The assistant belongs to its Space. When you switch Space at the top of the sidebar, you switch to that Space's assistant, its matters, what waits for you and its morning report. If Agents are still at work, The One asks first, because switching stops them (**Stop and switch**).

The assistant is an Agent like the others. Its role, `main-assistant`, is under **Teams and tools › Roles**. It works with your default model (**Settings › Models and sign-in › Default model**) unless you give that role a model preset of its own in a Space of yours. Its conversation lives on the home page, not in All runs.

Coming from 0.4 and wondering where your earlier sessions went? See the [0.5.0 release notes](../start/release-notes/0-5-0).

## What it does itself, and what becomes a matter

The assistant decides each time:

- What it can settle in seconds, it answers directly: a question, a quick look-up or status check, one small change.
- A follow-up, an addition or a change of mind about something already going on, it puts on that matter and passes to the working group on it.
- Work that produces something to deliver, runs an edit-and-test loop, gathers from several sources or will be followed over time becomes a new matter.

A new matter gets a short title and an overview that says where the work should get to, with every result you asked to see. The overview matters: deliveries are later held against it.

In the same step, the assistant hands the work to a working group. The group starts at once, and the assistant stays free for you and your other matters. It picks the group from the Space's working-group templates: software work in a code project goes to the software team (`polished_software_dev`), whose lead and checkers turn your requirements into tests as it builds; research and studio work goes to the template made for it; other multi-step work goes to the **General group** (`chat`). Among the templates that fit, it prefers one that has done this kind of work well. It has a new template written (by the **Definitions studio**, `atlas`) only when none fits and the work will come back.

See [Working-group templates](./capabilities/working-group-templates), [Matters and boards](./matters) and [Working groups](./working-groups).

## Your words, as you said them

When the assistant briefs a group, it copies your request into the brief word for word instead of retelling it. It adds only what the group cannot know: the project folder, a deadline, and what you said elsewhere that bears on the work. It does not tell the group how to split the work, what to test or which roles to use.

This is on purpose. A retold request carries the assistant's reading of it, and the group would settle the open points the way the assistant read them rather than the way you meant them. Follow-ups travel the same way: when you answer, correct or ask for more, the assistant passes your words on to the group already on the matter.

## While the groups work

The assistant keeps each open matter's one-line summary current: where it stands and whom it waits for. You see that line under the matter's title on its page, in the Matters overview when nothing more urgent needs saying, and in the sidebar when you hover over the matter. To see every group and its Agents at work right now, open the [Panorama](./panorama).

### Questions

A group's questions reach the assistant first. When the cards on the board, your earlier words or ordinary judgement settle one, it answers for you; when only you can decide, it leaves the question to you at once. It never makes up your answer. How long it has and when you are notified: see [Questions from working groups](./needs-you#questions-from-working-groups).

### Deliveries

When a group hands work back, the assistant holds it against what you asked for: each result you asked to see, and whether it stands up. An empty result, failing checks, or numbers from before the group's own fix fall short, however the group words them.

- When it falls short and the next step is the group's own (find out why, fix, rerun, add what is missing), the assistant sends the group back itself and tells you in a line.
- When the next step needs you (money, an account or key, a decision only you can make, restarting the app), it asks you one concrete question with the options.
- When it does what you asked, it tells you what came of it.

Its account starts with the conclusion against what you asked, not with "done": whether the work got there and what that means. Then come the numbers that matter, why it fell short when it did, and last the next step: who does what, and what you are to do now. The group's own words are on the board, so the assistant does not repeat them.

The verdict on each delivery is yours: the assistant never judges a delivery for you, and closing a matter is not a verdict. Where to press **Accept**, **Rework** or **Give up**, and how to give your verdict in your own words, is in [Judging a delivery](./needs-you#judging-a-delivery).

### Groups that stall

If a group's card still says it is working but nothing in it moves, or its members only retry an error, the assistant is told and decides what happens next. What it looks at and what it can do: see [Groups that stall](./working-groups#groups-that-stall).

### Quiet work

Work brought by a quiet trigger (one that is not set to tell you) goes on the matter's board without the assistant telling you, unless it falls short. See [Triggers and running in the background](./triggers).

## What it may do for you

The assistant holds every control you have on a matter's page. When you ask, it closes or reopens a matter, pauses, resumes or retries a group, and puts a matter's waiting work first. It sets or removes a matter's usage limit, and resumes work paused at one, only on your word. It also keeps cards on the boards: notes, checklists, links, files, readings and panels.

It has full authority over your work, including the sign-ins and saved keys on this computer and the spending the work needs. Nothing waits for your approval. It asks you only when it chooses to, for example when only you know something it needs. What keeps you in control is the record of everything Agents did and the undo that goes with it: see [Action log, undo and stopping](./action-log).

It can improve the Space itself too. Ask for a new template, role or Skill and it writes the definitions, itself or through the Definitions studio. Ask it to make the Space better at its own work and it opens the evolution matter (see [Evolution: the lab and the nightly review](./evolution)). Such changes take effect only in a Space of your own; in the read-only official Space they stay proposals. See [Definition Spaces](./capabilities/definition-spaces).

## The home page

The home page is the conversation with the main assistant. In a wide window an overview sits beside it (see below).

In a new Space where nothing was said yet, **How to start** sits above the box: a paragraph on what becomes of what you say (small things get a straight answer; work that takes effort becomes a matter, goes to a working group and comes back for your verdict), and four lines to start from (**Research**, **A page**, **A program**, **On a schedule**). Clicking one puts it in the box for you to change and send; nothing is sent for you.

### The sidebar

At the top of the sidebar are The One and the Space you are in; click the Space's name to switch to another. Below them come:

- **Conversation**: back to this conversation. The number beside it is how many things wait for you.
- **Panorama**: what the working groups on every open matter, and their Agents, are doing now. While Agents are at work it says how many, quietly, such as **3 at work**. See [Panorama](./panorama).
- **Start**: only until a model is set up; see [Quick Start](../start/quick-start).
- **Matters**: the heading says how many are open (such as **All 4**) and opens the Matters overview; under it is each open matter. **New matter: tell the assistant**, under the list, brings you back here with the cursor in the composer. See [Matters and boards](./matters).
- **Teams and tools**: what each working group does and which matters it works for, and the Plugins, Skills, roles and models. See [Teams and tools](./capabilities).
- **Apps**: only when a Plugin brings an app you can open; see [Plugins and Apps](./capabilities/plugins).
- **Settings**: see [Settings](./settings).

The Needs you page and the morning report are not in the sidebar: what waits for you is in the strip above the conversation, and the morning report opens from the day's greeting.

### The strip above the conversation

When something waits for you, a strip above the conversation says how many (for example **3 waiting for you**), with a button for each of the first three and the rest under **N more**. A button takes you to that thing's card in the conversation, where you answer, judge or fix it on the spot. The strip's number is the one beside **Conversation** in the sidebar. See [Waiting for you, deliveries and the morning report](./needs-you#waiting-for-you).

### Searching the conversation

**Search the conversation**, at the top right, or Cmd+F (Ctrl+F on Windows) opens a search above the conversation. It looks through what the conversation shows: your words, the assistant's, and the lines that say what happened on a matter. Hits come newest first; a click or Enter takes you to the message and makes it stand out, and Esc closes the search. It searches as far back as the conversation has loaded.

### The overview

In a window 1500 pixels wide or more, an overview sits to the right of the conversation and stays open; the overview button at the top right (an icon whose tooltip reads **Overview**) hides it or brings it back, and the app remembers your choice. In a narrower window it takes no room: the same button opens it over the conversation when you want it. It is not shown in the developer view. It has three parts, three rows at most each:

- **At work**: the working groups at work, the matter each works for and when it last moved. A row opens that matter.
- **Delivered lately**: the latest deliveries and which version each is, marked **Waiting for your verdict** while they wait for you. A row opens its evidence pack.
- **Coming up**: when the next triggers fire and for which matter, and any matter stopped at its spending limit. **All**, beside the heading, opens the trigger settings; while every trigger is paused the part says **Triggers are paused**.

Looking at the conversation marks what the assistant handed back as read.

### The day's greeting

When you open the app for the first time in a day, or come back to it on a new day after an hour or more away, and something happened that you have not seen, the main assistant greets you in the conversation: **Good morning** (or afternoon, or evening), what happened since you last looked, and how many things wait for you in the strip above. The greeting is the assistant's message at the time it greeted, so what you write next comes under it. **Open the morning report** opens the full report (see [The morning report](./needs-you#the-morning-report)), and **Got it** puts the greeting away for the day.

## Writing to it

**Enter** sends what you type; **Shift + Enter** starts a new line.

- Attach images or files with the paperclip (**Add image or file**), by dropping them on the conversation or by pasting. Images are compressed to fit 5 MB; other files can be up to 10 MB.
- **Templates** inserts a request template from your Space for you to fill in, such as **Implement a feature**, **Diagnose and fix a problem** or **Review and verify work**.
- The button beside **Send** shows your default model preset; open it to switch to another. See [Models](./capabilities/models).

Your own words show as your messages, including what you send from your phone (marked **From your phone**). The assistant's replies show in full; what it did on the way folds into one line of plain words that opens into one line per step. When it asks you something, the question is marked **Waiting for your answer**, and **Reply to this** makes what you send next the answer. A line above the first message of each day names the day: **Today** or **Yesterday** with its date, and the date alone for earlier days.

Messages that reached the assistant from elsewhere show as one line saying what happened, so they never mix with yours: a working group asked a question, handed something in, could not finish, keeps failing or stopped on a matter (**Open** goes to that matter), a trigger came due, or an outside program wrote. The line names the group in plain words, such as **Software team**. While the question or delivery it brought still waits for you, its card sits under the line, with the matter and the group it came from, and you answer or judge it there. Once it is handled, the card folds into one line where it was, saying what became of it: for example "You accepted delivery 1 of “Checkout redesign”" with **Look** for its evidence, or "The main assistant answered “Refund policy” for you" with the answer. On the latest delivery of a piece of work, **Change your mind** on that line brings back the verdicts it still allows.

While the assistant is busy, a line above the composer says what it is doing (thinking, at work, compacting its history or trying again), and **Stop** interrupts it; while it rests, the line is gone. When a reply did not get through, the message says why in plain words, for example that the model is too busy or that the official connection's allowance is used up, and the line says when it tries again, with **Try now** to try at once. To talk to the assistant from your phone, see [Phone channel and outside callers](./remote).

To see the assistant's system prompt, the notices as written for its model and each tool call's arguments, turn on the developer view; see [Settings](./settings).

## Before a model is set up

The assistant needs a model before it can start. Until you have connected a model service and picked a default model, the home page says **The main assistant has no model yet**, and the composer's button reads **Connect a model first**: it opens the step of **Get started** that is missing. You can still write down what you want. Your words are kept, and they wait in the assistant's composer once it can start. See [Quick Start](../start/quick-start).

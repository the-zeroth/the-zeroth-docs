The main assistant is the conversation The One opens on, and the one place to bring anything you want done. It answers what it can at once, keeps bigger work as matters, hands that work to working groups and follows it until you are satisfied.

## One conversation that goes on

Every Space has its own main assistant: **Assistant** in the sidebar, **Main assistant** on the page. It is one permanent conversation that runs across days and across all your matters. There is no new chat to start and no session list to tidy up. Everything you ask lands in the same place, so the assistant can connect what you said last week with what you ask today.

The assistant belongs to its Space. When you switch Space in the title bar, you switch to that Space's assistant, its matters, what needs you and its morning report. If Agents are still at work, The One asks first, because switching stops them (**Stop and switch**).

The assistant is an Agent like the others. Its role, `main-assistant`, is under **Capabilities › Roles**. It works with your default model (**Settings › Default model**) unless you give that role a model preset of its own in a Space of yours. Its conversation lives on the home page, not in **Settings › All runs**.

## What it does itself, and what becomes a matter

The assistant decides each time:

- What it can settle in seconds, it answers directly: a question, a quick look-up or status check, one small change.
- A follow-up, an addition or a change of mind about something already going on, it puts on that matter and passes to the working group on it.
- Work that produces something to deliver, runs an edit-and-test loop, gathers from several sources or will be followed over time becomes a new matter.

A new matter gets a short title and an overview that says where the work should get to, with every result you asked to see. The overview matters: deliveries are later held against it.

In the same step, the assistant hands the work to a working group. The group starts at once, and the assistant stays free for you and your other matters. It picks the group from the Space's working-group templates: software work in a code project goes to the software team (`polished_software_dev`), whose lead and checkers turn your requirements into tests as it builds; research and studio work goes to the template made for it; other multi-step work goes to the general `chat` group. Among the templates that fit, it prefers one that has done this kind of work well. It has a new template written (by the `atlas` group) only when none fits and the work will come back.

See [Working-group templates](./capabilities/working-group-templates), [Matters and boards](./matters) and [Working groups](./working-groups).

## Your words, as you said them

When the assistant briefs a group, it copies your request into the brief word for word instead of retelling it. It adds only what the group cannot know: the project folder, a deadline, and what you said elsewhere that bears on the work. It does not tell the group how to split the work, what to test or which roles to use.

This is on purpose. A retold request carries the assistant's reading of it, and the group would settle the open points the way the assistant read them rather than the way you meant them. Follow-ups travel the same way: when you answer, correct or ask for more, the assistant passes your words on to the group already on the matter.

## While the groups work

The assistant keeps each open matter's one-line summary current: where it stands and whom it waits for. You see that line under the matter's title, and in the sidebar when nothing more urgent needs saying.

### Questions

When a group asks something, the question shows on the matter's board at once, and the assistant gets the first chance at it. It answers when the cards, your earlier words or ordinary judgement settle it. When only you can decide (your taste, your money or accounts, a choice you have not made yet), or when you are the one talking to that group, it leaves the question to you and you are notified at once. It never makes up your answer. If it has done neither after three minutes, you are notified anyway, so no question waits unseen.

### Deliveries

When a group hands work back, the assistant holds it against what you asked for: each result you asked to see, and whether it stands up. An empty result, failing checks, or numbers from before the group's own fix fall short, however the group words them.

- When it falls short and the next step is the group's own (find out why, fix, rerun, add what is missing), the assistant sends the group back itself and tells you in a line.
- When the next step needs you (money, an account or key, a decision only you can make, restarting the app), it asks you one concrete question with the options.
- When it does what you asked, it tells you what came of it.

Its account starts with the conclusion against what you asked, not with "done": whether the work got there and what that means. Then come the numbers that matter, why it fell short when it did, and last the next step: who does what, and what you are to do now. The group's own words are on the board, so the assistant does not repeat them.

### Verdicts are yours

You judge each delivery with **Accept**, **Rework** or **Give up**, on the board, in Needs you or in the morning report (see [Needs you, deliveries and the morning report](./needs-you)). You can also just tell the assistant ("good", "change the colours", "drop it"): it records your verdict and, for a rework, passes your words to the group. It never judges a delivery for you, and closing a matter is not a verdict.

### Groups that stall

If a group's card still says it is working but nothing in it has moved for two minutes, or its Agents have only been retrying an error for ten, the assistant is told. It reads what the group last said and decides: tell the group what it lacks, give the work to another group or to other models, or tell you what is stuck. Only you can fix a model connection, its key or its usage limit; once you have, the assistant retries the group.

### Quiet work

Work brought by a quiet trigger (one that is not set to tell you) goes on the matter's board without the assistant telling you, unless it falls short. See [Triggers and running in the background](./triggers).

## What it may do for you

The assistant holds every control you have on a matter's page. When you ask, it closes or reopens a matter, pauses, resumes or retries a group, and puts a matter's waiting work first. It sets or removes a matter's usage limit, and resumes work paused at one, only on your word. It also keeps cards on the boards: notes, checklists, links, files, readings and panels.

It has full authority over your work, including the sign-ins and saved keys on this computer and the spending the work needs. Nothing waits for your approval. It asks you only when it chooses to, for example when only you know something it needs. What keeps you in control is the record of everything Agents did and the undo that goes with it: see [Action log, undo and stopping](./action-log).

It can improve the Space itself too. Ask for a new template, role or Skill and it writes the definitions, itself or through the `atlas` group. Ask it to make the Space better at its own work and it opens the evolution matter (see [Evolution: the lab and the nightly review](./evolution)). Such changes take effect only in a Space of your own; in the read-only official Space they stay proposals. See [Definition Spaces](./capabilities/definition-spaces).

## The home page

The conversation fills the left of the home page. Beside it:

- **Needs you**: matters paused at a usage limit you set, model connections only you can fix (with how many matters each holds up), then open questions, deliveries and runs that did not finish, newest first. It shows six at most; the rest are on the Needs you page. Answering here, on the matter's board or in the conversation is the same.
- **Going on**: the matters a working group is at, or that are hitting errors, each with its status line.
- **Since the last morning report**: how many deliveries, questions and unfinished runs came in since you last read it, with **Read the morning report** below.
- **Show closed** lists your closed matters, and **Open a matter yourself** opens one by hand.

Open a matter yourself when you want to collect notes before you hand something over. Give it a **Title**, say **What it is and where it should get to**, and you land on its board. The assistant sees it among its matters; tell it what to do with it. **New matter: tell the assistant**, under the matters in the sidebar, does the opposite: it brings you to the conversation with the cursor in the composer.

Looking at the conversation marks what the assistant handed back as read.

## Writing to it

**Enter** sends what you type; **Shift + Enter** starts a new line.

- Attach images or files with the paperclip (**Add image or file**), by dropping them on the conversation or by pasting. Images are compressed to fit 5 MB; other files can be up to 10 MB.
- **Templates** inserts a request template from your Space for you to fill in, such as **Implement a feature**, **Diagnose and fix a problem**, **Review and verify work** or **One-person company**.

Your own words show as your messages, including what you send from your phone (marked **From your phone**). Messages that reached the assistant from elsewhere show as cards that name their source, such as **Trigger** or **Matter notice** (with **Open this matter**), so they never mix with yours. To talk to the assistant from your phone, see [Phone channel and outside callers](./remote).

## Before a model is set up

The assistant needs a model before it can start. Until you have connected a model service and picked a default model, the home page says **The main assistant has no model yet**, and the composer's button reads **Connect a model first**: it opens the step of **Get started** that is missing. You can still write down what you want. Your words are kept, and they wait in the assistant's composer once it can start. See [Quick Start](../start/quick-start).

## Coming from 0.4

Up to 0.4.x you started a new chat for each task; that button is gone. The first time a Space has its main assistant, your earlier sessions move to **Archived**, at the bottom of Settings › All runs, where you can still open or restore them. Nothing is deleted, and sessions still at work or pinned stay where they were. To run a template yourself, outside any matter, use **New Graph** in Settings › All runs; see [All runs (developer view)](./settings/all-runs).

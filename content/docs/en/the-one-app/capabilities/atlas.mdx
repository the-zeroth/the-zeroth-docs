**Atlas** draws your whole Space on one canvas, so you can see how it fits together: which roles use which prompts, Skills and tools, which templates the roles work in, and which Plugins they rely on. The director at the bottom of the canvas turns a request in plain words into changes to the Space, made in front of you.

Open it with the **Atlas** tab above the lists of the editors for roles, prompts, Skills, external tools and templates; the page is titled **Definition Atlas**. With the developer view on, **Teams and tools** lists it too, under **In the developer view**.

![The Atlas canvas with a working-group template selected and the director's bar below](/docs-assets/images/0.5/en/atlas-canvas.png)

## The canvas

Each kind of definition has a zone of its own inside the **Space** frame: **Prompts**, **Skills**, **External tools** (MCP servers), **Roles** and **Graphs** (working-group templates). When roles use Plugins, a **Plugins** zone left of the frame, marked **Outside the Space**, holds the Plugins they use from outside the Space. Prompts, Skills and roles kept in folders are grouped by folder. Lines show who uses what and which role extends which.

- Select an item to light up everything connected to it. A prompt or Skill opens a panel with its full text, and an MCP server a panel that lists its tools; each says what uses it. A Plugin's panel says whether it is installed and how many places use it, opens the roles and templates that use it in their editors, and has **Open in Extension Center**.
- Zoom out for the shape of the whole Space and in for the details. The toolbar in the canvas corner zooms, has **Fit canvas** to fit everything on screen again, **Locate selection**, and the **Minimap**. Esc clears the selection and goes back to the whole map.
- **Find a definition…** jumps to any item by name.

## The director

The bar at the bottom of the canvas (**The Director**) takes a request in plain words, such as "add a reviewer to the research template" or "split this prompt in two". The director lays out its plan on the right and then makes the changes in front of you: the camera follows each edit, and the canvas stays locked until it is done. To stop it, choose **Interrupt assistant** on the bar.

The director writes and changes working-group templates, roles, prompts, Skills, external tools (MCP) and Plugins. It can also create or edit model presets, or switch the global default to another; model presets live on this computer and every Space shares them (see [Models](./models)).

The bar carries on your last Atlas conversation, so when you leave Atlas and come back, the director still knows where you were. **New chat** starts a fresh conversation; earlier ones stay under **Atlas Sessions** in All runs. See [All runs (developer view)](../settings/all-runs).

The director is a run of the built-in `atlas` working-group template, called **Definitions studio**, which every Space carries (see [Spaces](./definition-spaces)). You do not have to open Atlas to use it: ask the [main assistant](../main-assistant) for a new template or role, and it may make the change itself or give the work to the Definitions studio.

## Changes stay safe

The director changes the Space in one batch. It checks the whole Space before writing anything, and if the batch would break a reference, or the Space changed in the meantime, nothing is written. Every change it makes is recorded in **Settings › Data and undo › Action log**, where you can undo it. See [Action log, undo and stopping](../action-log).

Atlas can show the official Space, but changes need a Space of your own: in the read-only official Space, the director's edits are refused. See [Spaces](./definition-spaces).

A **Definition Space** is one complete set of what your Agents work with: working-group templates, roles, prompts, Skills, external tools (MCP), scripts, request templates and the Space's memory, together with the list of Plugins its roles use. You can keep as many Spaces as you like, for example one per client or kind of work, and they do not affect each other.

Manage them on the **Definition Spaces** page. The Space switcher at the top of the sidebar, beside **The One**, shows the active Space; pick another from its menu to switch. **Manage Spaces...** at the bottom of the menu opens the page. The page also sits beside **Plugins** in the Extension Center (**Teams and tools › Plugins**), and with the developer view on, **Teams and tools** lists it under **In the developer view**.

## What is in a Space, and what is on this computer

Everything in a Space is a file in the Space's folder:

- working-group templates, roles, prompts, Skills, the settings of external tools (MCP), and scripts;
- request templates, the ones in the composer's **Templates** menu;
- the Space's memory (see [Memory and recall](./memory));
- which Plugins its roles use, and those Plugins' settings that are not secret.

Switching Spaces swaps that whole set. Other things belong to the computer and every Space shares them:

- **Model presets and connections** (see [Models](./models)). A role or template that names a preset records the preset's ID, so when you share a Space, the other computer needs models that work too.
- **Installed Plugins**, the apps they bring, and their connections and keys (see [Plugins and Apps](./plugins)). Each Space chooses which Plugins it uses.
- **Settings › Data and undo › Action log**, which covers every Space so that undo can reach everything (see [Action log, undo and stopping](../action-log)).
- A few more things span Spaces: the weekly report's spend is the total for every Space; **Pause all** on the triggers pauses every Space's triggers; and what earlier sessions concluded in a project folder can be found from any Space (see [Memory and recall](./memory)).

Where does something new belong? Roles, prompts, templates and request templates belong to a Space. A capability with its own code, dependencies, settings, keys or app screen should be a Plugin, so every Space can use it and it can be updated on its own. A Skill or external tool that only makes sense in one Space can stay in that Space.

## What every Space carries

Every Space carries five built-in templates, with the roles, prompts and Skills they use:

| Template | Called in the app | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `main_assistant` | **Main assistant** | The [main assistant](../main-assistant) itself. The app keeps one run of it per Space. |
| `chat` | **General group** | One assistant that does ordinary multi-step work with tools directly. |
| `atlas` | **Definitions studio** | Its director writes and changes working-group templates, roles, prompts, Skills, external tools (MCP) and Plugins; the bar at the bottom of [Atlas](./atlas), **The Director**, runs it too. |
| `nightly_review` | **Nightly review** | Started each night at 03:30, only when there is something new to review. |
| `self_evolution_lab` | **Self-improvement lab** | The lab that works on evolution matters, with three roles: the Director, the Patcher and the Verifier. |

For the last two, see [Evolution: the lab and the nightly review](../evolution). Every Space also carries six built-in request templates, such as **Implement a feature**, **Diagnose and fix a problem** and **Create a plugin for The One**, which show in the composer's **Templates** menu.

When you create, duplicate or import a Space, and each time the app starts, The One adds any of these that are missing. Files that are already there are never overwritten, so your changes to them stay; it also means later improvements to these built-ins do not reach the copies you already have. Built-in templates are not in the template list, so Teams and tools shows a card for one only while it works for a matter.

A new, empty Space therefore starts with these five templates and nothing else, and with no external tool (MCP) servers. Until it has templates of its own, the main assistant gives ordinary work to the General group (`chat`).

## The official Space and your own copy

The One comes with the official Space, listed as **Default**. It is read-only: the app refreshes it on every launch, so it always carries the current version's templates, roles and Skills, and nothing else can change it. While you work in it:

- templates, roles, prompts, Skills and external tools (MCP) can be read but not changed;
- what the main assistant and the evolution lab find to improve stays a proposal (see [Evolution: the lab and the nightly review](../evolution));
- Agents cannot write memory;
- Plugins can be installed on this computer but do not reach the official Space: you cannot add them to its roles, and their tools and apps are not available there.

Model presets are not affected, since they live on this computer.

So make it your own. Three places copy it:

- **Copy the official Space and use it** in **Get started**, and **Duplicate to edit** in the notice at the top of the role, prompt and Skill editors, copy it and switch to the copy at once;
- **Duplicate to edit** on the official Space's row of the Definition Spaces page makes the copy; choose **Switch here** to start using it.

You, the main assistant and the evolution lab can change the copy freely. The cost is that later updates to the official Space do not reach your copy by themselves; when you upgrade The One, new built-ins are still added (see above).

Each Space in the list carries a badge: **Current** for the active one, **Read-only** or **Editable** for the others.

## What follows the active Space

Only one Space is active at a time, and the app shows that Space's work:

- the main assistant and its conversation: each Space has its own;
- matters, working groups and what waits for you;
- triggers: another Space's triggers wait until you switch back to it, and then by default catch up once;
- the weekly report and the nightly review;
- the runs in All runs.

## Switch, create, duplicate and delete

- **Switch here**, or the switcher at the top of the sidebar, changes the active Space at once, without a restart. If Agents are still at work, The One says how many and offers **Stop and switch**: they all stop first, and the step each one is on is cut off, not finished.
- **New Space** starts an empty Space with only what every Space carries (above).
- **Duplicate** copies a Space together with its memory and its choice of Plugins.
- **Edit** renames a Space of your own or changes its description.
- **Delete** removes a Space of your own, with all of its definitions and memory, for good. The active Space and the official one cannot be deleted.

## Export and import

**Export** packs a Space into a `.zerospace` file. Keys, tokens and passwords in its MCP settings are removed first. **Include agent memory** is on by default; turn it off to leave the memory out. See [Memory and recall](./memory).

**Import** reads a `.zerospace` file and shows what is inside before anything is written: how many roles, prompts, templates, MCP servers and Skills it holds, which scripts its Agents could run after the import, which MCP servers would run a command on your computer or connect elsewhere, and anything that fails validation, which stops the import. When the Space contains scripts or local commands, the button reads **I trust the source. Import**: import only Spaces from sources you trust.

A Space carries references to the Plugins its roles use, never the Plugins themselves. After an import, the Plugins page lists what the new Space's roles still need, so you can install the ones that run on your computer. See [Plugins and Apps](./plugins).

## Share on the website

**Share to platform** publishes a Space to the sharing platform on the-zeroth.com. Sharing needs you signed in to your account in The One (**Settings › General**).

- **Visibility**: **Private** is chosen for a first share; **Unlisted** makes it reachable by its link, and **Public** lists it for everyone.
- You can add a few tags, separated by commas.
- **Include agent memory** is off by default, so your notes and project context stay on your computer unless you turn it on.
- Keys, tokens and passwords in MCP settings, including those inside addresses and connection strings, are removed before the upload, and the platform refuses a bundle that still contains one.

Sharing the same Space again uploads a new version: the dialog starts from the last visibility and tags, and the visibility you choose this time applies. You need a username, set in the console on the website, before a share link can be made.

To use a Space someone else shared, choose **Browse sharing platform**, find the Space on [the-zeroth.com](https://the-zeroth.com/en/spaces) and choose **Open in The One**. The app downloads it and shows the same preview as an import from a file.
